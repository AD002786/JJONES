Questions

Before you book

Does it cost more to buy through an agent?

No. Premiums are set by the carrier and filed with the state, so you pay the same price whether you buy direct or through me. Agents are paid by the insurance company.

What does "independent agent" mean?

I'm appointed with several insurance companies instead of just one, so I can compare their plans and recommend the one that fits you best.

Can I get life insurance without a medical exam?

Often, yes. Many carriers offer simplified-issue and no-exam policies. Whether it's the best choice depends on your age, health and how much coverage you need.

When can I sign up for health insurance?

Most people enroll during open enrollment. A qualifying life event like losing coverage, moving, marriage or a new baby opens a special enrollment period, usually 60 days long.

I'm turning 65. When should I call?

About three months before your 65th birthday. Your Initial Enrollment Period runs from three months before your birthday month to three months after.
